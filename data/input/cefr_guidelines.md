# CEFR-aligned guidelines (authoritative local reference)

This document is the **local authoritative knowledge base** for curriculum grounding.  
Levels covered below: **A1**, **A2**, **B1** for **English** and **Spanish**.  
Use only structures and topic areas documented here when asserting what learners *can* do at each band.

---

## English

### A1

**Can-do summary (indicative):** Very basic interaction; familiar concrete needs; slow, rehearsed production.

**Grammar structures (authoritative examples for this level)**

- **Present simple** (affirmative/negative/question): *I work here. / She doesn’t speak Spanish. / Do you live in Madrid?*
- **Verb “to be”** for identity and location: *I am a student. / We are at home.*
- **Existential “there is/there are”**: *There is a book. / There are two chairs.*
- **Possessive determiners**: *my, your, his, her, our, their*
- **Basic word order**: SVO; questions with *do/does* or inversion with *to be*
- **Articles**: *a/an/the* in simple patterns
- **Numbers, dates, time** (telling time on the hour / simple schedules)

**Vocabulary topics (authoritative themes)**

- Personal information (name, age, nationality, job)
- Numbers, days, months, simple routines
- Family and basic descriptions (simple adjectives)
- Food and drink; ordering in a café (fixed phrases)
- Places in town; transport (bus, train); simple directions (*left/right*)
- Home and furniture; classroom objects

---

### A2

**Can-do summary (indicative):** Routine exchanges; simple connected text; short messages on familiar matters.

**Grammar structures (authoritative examples for this level)**

- **Present continuous** vs **present simple** contrast: *I’m studying* vs *I study*
- **Past simple** (regular/irregular, common verbs): *went, had, saw*
- **Going to** and **will** for simple future intentions
- **Countable/uncountable nouns**; *some/any*; **quantifiers** (*a few, a little*)
- **Comparatives and superlatives**: *bigger than / the most important*
- **Modal “can/can’t”** for ability/permission; **must/have to** for obligation (basic)
- **Conjunctions**: *and, but, because, when*
- **Object pronouns**: *me, you, him, her, us, them*

**Vocabulary topics (authoritative themes)**

- Hobbies, free time, sports (basic)
- Travel and holidays; booking; airports/trains (simple)
- Health (basic symptoms); pharmacy needs
- Shopping and prices; clothes and sizes
- Weather; seasons; environment (basic)
- Work tasks and workplace roles (simple)

---

### B1

**Can-do summary (indicative):** Main points of clear input on familiar matters; simple connected argument; travel situations.

**Grammar structures (authoritative examples for this level)**

- **Present perfect** (experience/result): *I’ve never been to … / I’ve just finished*
- **Past continuous** in narrative: *I was working when …*
- **First conditional**; introduction to **second conditional** (hypothetical but stable patterns)
- **Reported speech** (basic backshift): *He said (that) he was tired*
- **Passive voice** (common forms): *It is made in … / The letter was sent*
- **Relative clauses** (defining): *The person who … / The book that …*
- **Phrasal verbs** (high-frequency, limited set at this band)
- **Discourse markers**: *however, although, because of, in order to*

**Vocabulary topics (authoritative themes)**

- Opinions and arguments (agreeing/disagreeing politely)
- Media and news headlines (general topics)
- Education and training; skills and goals
- Environment and society (non-specialist)
- Work interviews and CV-related language (basic)

## Spanish

### A1

**Can-do summary (indicative):** Interacciones muy básicas; necesidades inmediatas; producción lenta y formulaica.

**Grammar structures (authoritative examples for this level)**

- **Presente de indicativo** (regular *-ar/-er/-ir*): *hablo, como, vivo*
- **Ser / estar / hay**: identidad, ubicación, existencia — *Soy estudiante. / Estoy en casa. / Hay dos mesas.*
- **Artículos** *el/la/los/las* y omisión controlada en patrones fijos
- **Género y número** en sustantivos y adjetivos básicos
- **Preguntas** con entonación y palabras interrogativas: *qué, quién, dónde, cuándo, cómo*
- **Negación** *no* y orden básico de palabras
- **Números, fechas, hora** (en punto; *y cuarto / y media* de forma básica)

**Vocabulary topics (authoritative themes)**

- Datos personales; nacionalidades; profesiones básicas
- Familia; descripciones simples
- Comida y bebida; restaurante (frases fijas)
- Ciudad; direcciones simples (*izquierda/derecha/recto*)
- Casa y objetos cotidianos; clase y materiales escolares

---

### A2

**Can-do summary (indicative):** Intercambios rutinarios; textos breves conectados; mensajes sobre temas familiares.

**Grammar structures (authoritative examples for this level)**

- **Presente** habitual vs **estar + gerundio** (*estoy estudiando*)
- **Pretérito indefinido** (pretérito perfecto simple) para hechos acabados; verbos frecuentes irregulares
- **Ir a + infinitivo** y **pensar + infinitivo** para planes próximos
- **Gustar-type verbs** (*me gusta / me interesa*)
- **Demostrativos** (*este/ese/aquel*) y **posesivos** (*mi/tu/su…*)
- **Comparativos** (*más… que*, *menos… que*, *tan… como*)
- **Conectores básicos**: *y, pero, porque, cuando*
- **Pronombres de objeto** (*me, te, lo, la…*) en posiciones básicas

**Vocabulary topics (authoritative themes)**

- Aficiones; tiempo libre; deportes (básico)
- Viajes; transporte; hotel (básico)
- Salud (síntomas sencillos); farmacia
- Compras y precios; ropa y tallas
- Tiempo atmosférico; estaciones

---

### B1

**Can-do summary (indicative):** Puntos principales de textos claros sobre asuntos familiares; viajes; opiniones simples razonadas.

**Grammar structures (authoritative examples for this level)**

- **Pretérito indefinido** vs **imperfecto** en narración (*cuando era niño… / ayer fui…*)
- **Presente perfecto compuesto** (*he vivido*, *hemos terminado*) según norma peninsular estándar
- **Futuro simple / ir a** para predicción y planes; **condicional simple** de cortesía (*me gustaría*)
- **Subjuntivo presente** en patrones fijos (*quiero que…, es importante que…*)
- **Pasiva refleja** (*se vende*, *se habla español*)
- **Oraciones relativas** (*que, quien, el cual* — uso controlado)
- **Conectores** (*sin embargo, además, aunque, por eso*)

**Vocabulary topics (authoritative themes)**

- Opinión y debate sencillo (acuerdo/desacuerdo educado)
- Noticias generales; temas de actualidad no especializados
- Educación; objetivos y habilidades
- Medio ambiente y sociedad (no especializado)
- Trabajo; entrevistas básicas y CV (léxico general)
